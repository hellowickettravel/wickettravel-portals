Wicket Travel · Design System · v2

Solid, compact, and the orange you can actually see.

Version 1 shipped three mistakes: gradients, an asymmetric corner, and an accent so muted it vanished. All three are corrected here. This version replaces v1 entirely.

01 — Corrections

What was wrong, and what replaces it

These are not preferences. They are defects in v1 that made finished screens look cheap.

Remove — v1
  • Gradient sidebar — a colour fading into another colour reads as a template.
  • Gradient stat cards — tint fading to white made every card look washed and cheap.
  • The clipped corner (5/18/18/18) — intended as a signature, reads as a rendering glitch.
  • Accent #C0451F — brown, muted, and invisible on screen.
  • 46px buttons at 22px padding — wide and heavy.
  • Visible scrollbars inside the sidebar and panels.
Adopt — v2
  • Solid fills only. No gradient anywhere in the product, ever.
  • Solid tint cards with a matching 1px border — flat, crisp, confident.
  • One radius per element type — 14 cards, 10 controls, 7 chips, 11 icon chips.
  • Flame #D24417 — vivid, warm, and it is now the primary action colour.
  • 42px buttons at 18px padding — compact and purposeful.
  • No scrollbar is ever visible inside the app. Hard rule.
02 — Scrolling

No scrollbar is ever visible

A native scrollbar sitting inside the sidebar is the single most brand-destroying detail on the screen — it announces that the layout was never designed. This is a hard rule with no exception.

PageThe document scrolls as one. Do not create nested scroll containers for layout.
Sidebarposition:sticky, top 0, height 100dvh, overflow-y auto — with the scrollbar hidden. If the nav is long, it scrolls silently.
CSSscrollbar-width:none · -ms-overflow-style:none · ::-webkit-scrollbar{display:none} — applied to every internal scroll area
TablesNever scroll horizontally on mobile. They become stacked cards instead.
Modals & chatScroll silently. No visible track, no arrows, no inset shadow at the edges.
BannedNative scrollbars, custom styled scrollbars, scroll arrows, "scroll for more" cues, nested scroll inside a card
Test for it: open every screen at 1280px and at 375px. If a track or thumb appears anywhere other than the browser's own page scrollbar, it is a bug — not a styling choice.
03 — Colour

Blue builds the room. Orange does the work.

In v1 the orange was reserved so tightly it never appeared. Now flame is the primary action colour — every main button, every active icon — so the brand is visible on every screen while blue still holds the structure.

04 — Radius

Consistent, and slightly ours

The clipped corner is gone. Distinctiveness comes from the colour discipline, the mono codes and the fare stub — not from a corner that looks broken. Radii are consistent per element type, and deliberately off the framework defaults of 8 / 12 / 16.

14pxCards, panels, stat cards, table containers, images
18pxLarge blocks — modals, the sidebar, hero panels, auth panels
10pxButtons, inputs, selects, nav items
11pxIcon chips and the monogram badge
7pxBadges and tags — squared, never a pill
CircleAvatars and status dots only
BannedAsymmetric radii · 16px on everything · pill buttons · pill eyebrows

10 — Ship checklist

Run every screen past this

FAIL Reject the screen if

  • Any gradient appears — sidebar, card, button, panel or overlay.
  • A scrollbar is visible anywhere except the browser's own page scrollbar.
  • An asymmetric or 16px-everywhere radius is used.
  • The screen has no flame on it — the brand accent must appear at least once.
  • Buttons stretch to fill their container outside an auth card.
  • A stat card has a shadow or a different padding from its neighbour.
  • Status colours vary by screen — money must be gold everywhere, pending violet everywhere.
  • A table scrolls sideways on mobile instead of stacking.

PASS The screen is ready when

  • Every fill is one flat colour, and separation comes from 1px borders.
  • Scrolling is silent at 1280px and 375px.
  • Radii are 14 / 18 / 10 / 11 / 7 by element type, circles only for avatars.
  • The primary action is flame and there is exactly one per view.
  • Every number in a metric, fare or column is tabular.
  • Icons are Lucide at 1.75, in tinted chips that match the metric's meaning.
  • Type follows the scale and body sits at #3A4A5C, 1.68 line-height.
  • Tables stack into cards below 768px with 44px tap targets.
