# Wicket Travel — Homepage Structure Brief (Cleaned)

Purpose: structural/element reference only, scanned from the current live homepage (wickettravel.com), top to bottom. This is NOT a design spec — no colors, fonts, sizes, spacing, or alignment are specified anywhere in this file. Use it only to understand what elements exist in each section and roughly how they're grouped (grid vs list vs carousel). Layout, visual style, and art direction are open — design freely, taking cues from the redesigned client portal (portal.wickettravel.com) for the overall look and feel.

All imagery should be sourced from stock photography (travel/destination/lifestyle themed) unless otherwise noted.

Note: this version trims sections that were repeating the same content/message more than once on the original page (see "Cleanup notes" at the bottom for what was merged or removed and why).

## Cleanup notes (what changed from the raw scan, and why)

- **Airline logo strip** folded into the stats section instead of its own section — it was sitting right next to two other airline/trust sections (stats + featured airlines) saying the same thing three times in a row.
- **"Handpicked Hotels & Resorts" grid removed as a standalone section** — hotels were already being shown in the Holidays cross-promo section and again in Featured Deals. Kept one small hotel highlight grid inside the cross-promo section so hotels aren't dropped entirely, just not repeated 3 times.
- **"Trust & Security" merged into "Why Wicket Travel"** — both sections were listing near-identical points (trusted/verified airlines, no hidden fees/transparent pricing, best fares/best-price guarantee) under two different headings back to back.
- **"Community, live" merged into "Parents Tickets"** — it was a standalone section entirely dependent on Parents Tickets for context; kept as a supporting element inside that section instead of a separate one.
- **Left untouched but flagging for your call:** the Dubai Visa section still carries the full 5-step embedded form. It's the heaviest single block on the homepage — I didn't touch it since it looked like a deliberate lead-gen funnel (a real business decision, not a duplication issue), but if you want it lighter, it could become a short teaser + CTA linking to a dedicated visa page instead of the full form living on the homepage. Let me know if you want that trimmed too.
- Everything else (hero, browse & book, region fares, deals feed, phone/WhatsApp banner, testimonials, FAQ, newsletter, footer) was left as-is — no repeated content, all pulling their own weight.
