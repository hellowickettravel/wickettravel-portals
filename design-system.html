01 — The shift

From assertive to settled

The previous system shouted: heavy weights, tight tracking, hard corners on everything. This one is quieter and more confident — the kind of interface people can sit inside for eight hours without feeling shouted at.

Softer type, wider air

Weights stop at 700. Tracking sits near zero. Line-heights are generous. The page reads calm before you've read a word of it.

One corner tells the story

Every surface carries a small clipped top-left corner — a filed document, a stamped ticket. It's the detail nobody else has.

Text has five colours, not one

Headings, body, muted, faint and inverted are each defined. Pure black never appears; neither does full-strength contrast on long paragraphs.

02 — Typefaces

Three faces, chosen for temperament

Archivo was strong but sharp — a grotesk built to command. Hanken Grotesk does the same work with open apertures and softer terminals, so it holds a page steady instead of pushing at it.

Hanken Grotesk
Everything — headings, body, UI
Aa Hyderabad

Humanist grotesk with generous x-height and open counters. Legible at 13px, dignified at 44px. Weights 400 · 500 · 600 · 700 — nothing heavier, ever.

Newsreader italic
Editorial accent, used sparingly
the best fare, found

A warm, low-contrast serif italic for one moment per page — a pull quote, a promise, a line in the hero. Never for headings, labels or UI.

IBM Plex Mono
Codes, references, micro-labels
LHR → HYD

Airport codes, booking references, timestamps and every uppercase micro-label. Weight 500 only.

Why not a second display face: a booking product is 90% interface — forms, tables, cards. One grotesk across the whole system keeps it coherent, and the serif italic earns its place precisely because it appears once.

04 — Radius language

The clipped corner

Uniform roundness is the most common tell of a generated interface. Ours is asymmetric and derived from the product: every surface has one small corner, top-left, as though the document had been clipped and filed.

Surface5 · 18 · 18 · 18
Large surface6 · 24 · 24 · 24
Control8 all round
Chip / badge6 all round
Icon chip12 all round
Avatar onlyFull circle
Cards & panels5px 18px 18px 18px
Feature blocks6px 24px 24px 24px — heroes, modals, dark bands
ImagesSame as their container — the clip carries through
Buttons & inputs8px uniform — controls stay symmetrical so they read as controls
Badges & tags6px — squared, like a printed label. Never a pill.
Icon chips12px uniform
CirclesAvatars and status dots only
Banned16px-on-everything · rounded-2xl · pill buttons · pill eyebrows
Why top-left: it's the corner the eye lands on first in a left-aligned layout, so the detail registers without needing to be large. Applied at 5px against 18px, it reads as craft rather than as a shape.

06 — Spacing

A 4px grid, applied without exception

Most of the "it feels off" in an interface is spacing that doesn't repeat. These values repeat.

Container1160px max · 40px gutters desktop · 24px mobile

Card padding26px 28px desktop · 20px 22px mobile

Label → input8px
Field → field22px
Input → error8px
Button row gap12px
Icon → label9px in buttons · 13px in nav
The rhythm rule: space between groups is always larger than space inside a group. If a label looks like it belongs to the field below it rather than above it, the gaps are wrong — not the design.
07 — Icons

One family, one weight, always meaningful

Mixed icon sets are the fastest way a product starts to look assembled from parts. Everything comes from Lucide at 1.75px — slightly more substantial than the hairline default, which keeps icons legible at 18px without turning them into blocks.

Librarylucide-react, exclusively. No emoji. No filled icons. No second set.
Stroke1.75px at every size
Sizes16 inline · 18 buttons · 20 nav & chips · 24 page headers
ColourInherits text colour. Inside a chip it takes the semantic hue.
Chip42px, radius 12px, hue at 9–16% background
AlignmentOptically centred, never baseline-aligned with text
AloneOnly in icon-buttons, always with an aria-label

Chips carry meaning, not decoration

Ocean for volume · amber for money · indigo for waiting · mint for confirmed · rose for attention · coral for featured. The hue is fixed by meaning and reused on every screen.

11 — Page rhythm

How a section is assembled

Every section, marketing or portal, follows the same five-part order — and no two neighbouring sections share a shape.

1 · EyebrowPlex Mono 11px, coral-deep, carrying a fact — a date, a count, a route
2 · HeadingH2, left-aligned, one line where possible
3 · LedeOne or two sentences, max 58 characters wide
4 · ContentThe actual thing — stubs, cards, a table, a form
5 · CloseOne action, or nothing
BackgroundAlternate canvas → white → sky tint. Ocean band once or twice per page.
ColumnsVary: 2-up, then a wide single, then 3-up. Four identical 3-column grids is the tell.
AlignmentLeft by default. Centre only inside an ocean statement band.
Editorial lineOne Newsreader italic moment per page, maximum
DensityMarketing pages breathe; portal screens tighten to 72px sections and 20px card padding

13 — Ship checklist

Run every screen past this

RETIRE Never again

  • Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter or Poppins — the three most recognisable generated-interface faces.
  • #F97316 or any framework default colour value.
  • Uniform 16px radius on every element, or pill-shaped buttons and eyebrows.
  • Weight 800 with tight tracking — it reads as shouting, not confidence.
  • Pure black or near-black body text on long paragraphs.
  • Borderless inputs that only appear when focused.
  • The same shadow on every element, so nothing reads as more important.
  • Emoji as icons, or two icon libraries in one product.
  • Centred heading + three identical cards, repeated down the page.
  • "Seamless", "elevate", "unlock", "revolutionise" anywhere in the copy.

ADOPT Always

  • Hanken Grotesk 400–700, Newsreader italic once per page, Plex Mono for codes.
  • The clipped corner — 5/18/18/18 on every surface, 8px on controls, 6px on badges.
  • Five text colours: #0A3A66 · #37485C · #6B7C8E · #98A6B5 · #FFFFFF.
  • Line-height 1.7 on body and tracking at or near zero.
  • Visible input borders at rest, with a real inner shadow.
  • Lucide at 1.75px, in tinted chips that match the metric's meaning.
  • Alternating section backgrounds and varied column counts.
  • Errors that say what to do next.
  • One coral action per view — everything else is ocean or secondary.
  • Copy that states a fact: "We hold the fare while you check with the family."
14 — Implementation

Set the tokens, then build

No component file should ever contain a raw hex or a one-off pixel value.

Fonts — root layout

// replaces Plus Jakarta Sans / Archivo
import { Hanken_Grotesk, Newsreader, IBM_Plex_Mono }
  from "next/font/google"

const sans = Hanken_Grotesk({ subsets:["latin"],
  weight:["400","500","600","700"],
  variable:"--font-sans" })

const serif = Newsreader({ subsets:["latin"],
  style:["italic"], weight:["400"],
  variable:"--font-serif" })

const mono = IBM_Plex_Mono({ subsets:["latin"],
  weight:["500"], variable:"--font-mono" })
